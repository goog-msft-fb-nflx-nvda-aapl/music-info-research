What DEAP is: "Database for Emotion Analysis using Physiological Signals" (Queen Mary University of London, 2012). 32 people watched 40 one-minute music videos while 32-channel EEG (electroencephalography, brain activity recorded from the scalp) was recorded; each person rated every video for valence (unpleasant to pleasant) and arousal (calm to excited) on a 1-9 scale.
Page: https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/mmv/datasets/deap/
Access: a signed license form (EULA = End User License Agreement) is emailed to the owners, who then send a download login. The data cannot be shared between people outside that agreement, so I will not accept a copy unless you are covered by the same license.
