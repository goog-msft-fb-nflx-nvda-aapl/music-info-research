## Important caveat on “mirrors”

Do **not** use unofficial Kaggle, Hugging Face, GitHub, or cloud-drive copies of DEAP, AMIGOS, MAHNOB-HCI, or SEED unless the uploader is demonstrably the dataset owner or an authorized repository. For DEAP, the only Kaggle item found explicitly states that it contains derived data and that “the raw dataset is licensed and must be obtained separately from the original source under its EULA.”  For SEED, the license expressly forbids distribution of the dataset or portions of it and forbids sharing the issued access link. [kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/har07sudhan/deap-dataset)

## Fastest route for a student team in Taiwan

1. **MET (OpenNeuro ds008701)** — fastest if you need music-induced **valence and arousal** ratings. First step: create/log in to OpenNeuro and download [https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds008701/versions/1.0.5](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds008701/versions/1.0.5). [openneuro](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds008701/versions/1.0.5)
2. **ds002721 / ds002724** — fastest if CC-BY licensing and published derivative figures/models matter. First step: download ds002721 from [https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds002721/versions/1.0.3](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds002721/versions/1.0.3); it is CC BY 4.0. [openneuro](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds002721/versions/1.0.3)
3. **DREAMER** — fastest among the classic emotion-EEG benchmarks. First step: create a Zenodo account and submit the access request at [https://zenodo.org/records/546113](https://zenodo.org/records/546113), including name, email, institution, position, and reason. [zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/546113)
4. **SEED / SEED-IV / SEED-V** — strong labeled EEG corpora, but a faculty PI must complete and upload the signed license; first step: have your supervisor download and sign [https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/home/seed/resource/license/license.pdf](https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/home/seed/resource/license/license.pdf). [bcmi.sjtu.edu](https://bcmi.sjtu.edu.cn/home/seed/resource/license/license.pdf)
5. **DEAP** — valuable but slowest for a student-only team because the applicant must hold a permanent academic/research position and use an institutional email. First step: ask a permanent faculty member at your Taiwanese institution to submit the DEAP request via [https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/mmv/datasets/deap/download.html](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/mmv/datasets/deap/download.html). [eecs.qmul.ac](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/mmv/datasets/deap/download.html)
6. **AMIGOS and MAHNOB-HCI** — do not rely on mirrors. First step: retry the official AMIGOS page and MAHNOB portal; both were not verifiable live in this check, so their current forms, eligibility, and exact license wording remain **UNVERIFIED**. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13290899/)
