PROJECT (what we want to build)
Title (working): Transferring EEG-based emotion modelling across datasets for emotion-conditioned music accompaniment (a foundation for adaptive music biofeedback in music therapy).

Motivation: Music therapy uses music to influence emotional state. Adaptive biofeedback systems are limited by the scarcity of high-quality paired EEG + audio data that also carries emotion labels. We ask whether emotion modelling learned on one paired EEG-emotion dataset can be transferred to another paired EEG-music dataset, in order to generate emotion-conditioned music accompaniment.

Datasets named by the team:
- NMED-T (Stanford): continuous 128-channel EEG and audio, 20 participants, 10 full-length songs.
- DEAP (QMUL): 32-channel EEG (+ peripheral signals), 32 participants, 40 one-minute music-video excerpts, per-trial valence/arousal/dominance/liking self-ratings (1-9).

Proposed pipeline:
Phase 1. Train an EEG encoder (e.g. CNN-Transformer) on DEAP to regress continuous valence-arousal (VA) with an MSE objective. Evaluate with leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) cross-validation.
Phase 2. Harmonize NMED-T's 128 channels to DEAP's 32-channel montage by spatial interpolation. Run the frozen DEAP encoder on harmonized NMED-T to produce VA pseudo-labels, keep predictive uncertainty, and filter low-confidence samples.
Phase 3. Emotion-conditioned accompaniment transformation: fine-tune a pretrained latent diffusion audio backbone (not from scratch). The original audio gives musical context; separately encoded EEG embeddings and VA embeddings give emotional conditioning via cross-attention. Modality dropout enables EEG-only, VA-only and joint conditioning at inference. Investigate emotion-aware generation objectives (alignment between target emotion and generated audio).
Evaluation: Frechet Audio Distance (FAD), spectral characteristics, musical coherence, and MSE between the emotion predicted on generated audio (or from EEG) and the target VA coordinates.

Compute: 4x H200 (shared), Hugging Face and GitHub access. No human listening test (quantitative evaluation only). Code and audio demos must be public and reproducible.
