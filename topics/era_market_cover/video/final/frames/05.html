Phase 2 · generation

The cover system

  • Keep the original vocal; regenerate the accompaniment with MusicGen-Melody.
  • Input: chroma of the original mix and a text prompt for the era.
  • A low-rank adapter trained on training accompaniment, prompt = the clip's decade.

Three-branch guidance

logits = null + gm (neutral − null) + ge (era − neutral)

The melody term gm = 3 stays, so the song stays recognisable; the era term ge pushes toward the decade. Contrast-only guidance lowers song identification from .875 to about .67.
