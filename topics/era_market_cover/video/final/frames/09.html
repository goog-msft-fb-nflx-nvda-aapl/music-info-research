What limits the claim

  • Bandwidth: prompts with no decade, only “dull, band-limited, tape hiss”, shift the probes by about half the 1960s prompt effect (MERT −1.34 vs −2.79 expected decades).
  • Spectrum matching keeps the MERT 1980s and 2010s effects positive; the MuQ 2010s effect halves.
  • Wording: for the 1990s, a grunge wording gives the weakest effect and a eurodance wording the strongest in MERT; no wording wins in both encoders.
  • Other generators: MuseControlLite keeps the melody but barely moves the probes; ACE-Step moves them but keeps much less of the song.
