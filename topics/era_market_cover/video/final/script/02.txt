We use thirty second clips from Discogs VI recordings. Dataset A has six release decades, from the nineteen sixties to the twenty tens. Dataset B has six release markets, all from the nineteen eighties: United States, United Kingdom, Brazil, Spain, Germany and Italy. Test labels are hidden, so every accuracy is on the validation split, with a hundred thirty two and a hundred two clips. One caution about labels: in the full Discogs VI metadata, a composition has on average two point four two release countries and almost two release decades. So these labels describe editions, not compositions.
