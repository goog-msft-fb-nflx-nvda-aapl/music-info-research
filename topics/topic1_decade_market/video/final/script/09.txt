Several controls limit the claims. First, prompts that name no decade, only bandwidth, such as dull, band limited, with tape hiss, shift the probes by about half the effect of the sixties prompt. So part of the era effect rides on bandwidth. Second, matching the long term spectrum of each clip to its neutral clip keeps the MERT eighties and twenty tens effects positive, but halves the MuQ twenty tens effect. Third, wording matters. For the nineties, a grunge wording gives the weakest effect and a eurodance wording the strongest in MERT, and no wording wins in both encoders. Two other generators behave differently: MuseControlLite keeps the melody but barely moves the probes, and ACE Step moves them but keeps much less of the song.
