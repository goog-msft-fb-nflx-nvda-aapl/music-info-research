What the lecture on MIDI-to-audio adds

  • Relevant: symbolic melody as the condition instead of chroma, as in recent work that conditions a pop generator on transcribed MIDI.
  • Relevant: the monophonic note-to-audio problem is singing voice synthesis, which fits the target-language vocal of the market axis.
  • Not relevant: classical synthesizer types and single-note neural models.
