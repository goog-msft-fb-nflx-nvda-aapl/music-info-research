Evaluation

How quality is assessed and compared

  • Controllability: probe index and gain, R-Precision and multimodal distance, dose monotonicity.
  • Distribution: FAD per cell, extrapolated to infinite sample size.
  • Variety: Diversity and MultiModality.
  • Content: song identification, chroma similarity.

Compared with previous work

  • Baselines re-run on the same 24 songs: text-only generation, melody plus text prompts, a melody adapter, an era LoRA, training-free steering, best-of-N.
  • Numbers of other papers are not mixed in.
