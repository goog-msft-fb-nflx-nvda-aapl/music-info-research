Here is the control plane at the discrete stage. Rows are the era dose, columns are the language share, which is the continuous market control, here for Spanish. On the left, the era index of the mix changes down the rows and stays almost flat along the columns. On the right, the language probability changes along the columns and stays flat down the rows. So the two axes act separately. German behaves the same way. The market itself is still carried only by the sung language, not yet by a change of the music.
