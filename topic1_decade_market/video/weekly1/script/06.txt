On quality assessment. We use existing metrics only. For controllability: probe index and gain, R precision and multimodal distance in embedding space, adapted from the text to motion literature, and dose monotonicity. For distribution quality: FAD per cell against real clips of the decade, extrapolated to infinite sample size. For variety: diversity and multimodality. For content: song identification and chroma similarity. For comparison with previous work, we re run the available baselines on the same twenty four songs, and we do not mix numbers from other papers into our tables, since data, embeddings and references differ.
