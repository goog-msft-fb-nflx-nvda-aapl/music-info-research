We treat the task as content preserving style transfer, as in the course lecture on cover generation: content is melody, chords and lyric meaning, style is everything else. We split it into six parts. Content extraction. Singable lyric translation, where we do not yet constrain rhythm or syllables, and which we will measure next. Rendering the sung translation while keeping the singer. The accompaniment by era, which is done. The music by market beyond language, which is open and needs a language free market descriptor. And evaluation, where no agreed cover metric exists. We found no published system that combines translated lyrics, a metadata controlled accompaniment, and a preserved melody and singer. The closest are singing voice translation, cover generation with phoneme control, and singable lyric translation. We will repeat the survey after the next experiments.
