Compared with a control conditioner that uses the same route without the adapter bias, the adapter reaches the target decade at a lower dose. At dose point five the mean decade index is 3.25 for the adapter and 2.15 for the control. Between doses point three and point seven, the adapter is ahead on MERT and on EffNet in all three training seeds. But the distribution level metric, FAD with sample size extrapolation, does not rank the two models consistently across seeds. So our claim is that the adapter reaches the decade at a lower dose, not that it generates more realistic audio of the decade.
