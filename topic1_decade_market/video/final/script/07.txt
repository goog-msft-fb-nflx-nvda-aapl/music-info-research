Extending to six prompts, with the adapter the expected decade of the probe increases monotonically from the sixties prompt to the twenty tens prompt. This holds in both encoders, and in an independent third encoder. The unmodified model does not. Its nineteen seventies prompt even gives a later decade than the neutral prompt. The nineteen nineties prompt is the weakest. Thin dashed lines show two other adapter training seeds, which behave the same.
