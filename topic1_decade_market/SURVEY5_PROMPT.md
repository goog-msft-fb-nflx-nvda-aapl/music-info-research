# Survey round 6 prompt (paste as plain text into ChatGPT Deep Research and Gemini Deep Research; do not upload as a file)

I am writing a paper on cover-song generation with two controllable parameters: the release decade (era) and the release market (country or language region) of the recording. A pretrained music generator (MusicGen-Melody, ACE-Step) keeps the melody and chords of a given song, and the user chooses a point on a 2D plane (era x market) that sets the production style, instrumentation and vocal language. I know of no prior work that conditions generation on release decade or market. I want methods from ANY modality (image, video, 3D/human mesh, motion, speech, text) that transfer, because the problem is a general "control two attributes of a diffusion or autoregressive generator" problem.

Please research papers from 2023 to now (arXiv, ICLR/ICML/NeurIPS/CVPR/ICCV/ECCV/SIGGRAPH/ISMIR/ICASSP) and return, with verified titles, authors, venue, year and arXiv ids (mark anything you could not open as unverified):
1. Continuous multi-attribute control of pretrained generators: concept sliders, compositional sliders, steering vectors, activation steering, LoRA interpolation, prompt-embedding interpolation. For each: how the attribute is parameterised, how several attributes are combined, how interference between attributes is reduced.
2. Classifier-free guidance with several conditions: separate guidance scales per condition (InstructPix2Pix-style), composable diffusion, subset-weighted guidance, training with random condition dropping, independence assumptions and when they fail.
3. Conditioning on a continuous parameter vector: body shape and pose (SMPL / SMPL-X) in image and video diffusion, face attribute control, camera parameters, 3D generation. How the parameters enter the network (cross-attention, adapters, ControlNet), what happens when parameters are correlated.
4. Controlling a categorical or ordinal nuisance-prone attribute whose label is confounded with another attribute (here: market is confounded with language; decade is confounded with bandwidth and loudness): concept erasure (LEACE, INLP), adversarial or invariant conditioning, counterfactual data, causal or disentangled conditioning in generation.
5. Evaluation of attribute control: metrics for per-attribute accuracy, monotonic response to the control value, cross-attribute leakage, disentanglement with correlated attributes (dependency-aware MIG), controllability benchmarks, use of independent read-out models so the generator is not scored by the instrument it was selected with, and evaluation without human listening or rating tests.
6. Music-specific: style, genre, timbre, production-era or culture control in text-to-music and cover or accompaniment generation (MusicGen, Stable Audio, ACE-Step, MuseControlLite, Music ControlNet, JASCO, SongEcho and newer), and any work on release-year, era or regional-market effects in music audio.
For each group give 3 to 6 papers, one line on what transfers to a two-axis (era x market) cover-generation system, and one line on its main limitation. End with (a) a ranked list of the 5 techniques you would try first and why, (b) a ranked list of evaluation protocols I should adopt, (c) claims in this prompt you think are wrong or already published. Do not invent citations; say "not found" instead.
